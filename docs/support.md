# Support and feedback

For account or private support, email **[support@brainscanner.dev](mailto:support@brainscanner.dev)**.

For public product feedback, [open an issue](https://github.com/kevin-lozada-santos/brainscanner/issues). A useful report includes:

- What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
- The view or workflow involved.
- Your browser or agent client, if relevant.
- A short reproduction with synthetic details or a redacted screenshot.

Do not post passwords, access tokens, private source files, customer data, or private project links in a public issue. Use email for account-specific details, and keep sensitive content out of attachments.
